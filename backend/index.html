Understanding Your Vitals

Heart Rate (BPM)

Your heart rate measures how many times your heart beats per minute.

Normal resting range: 60 - 100 BPM

Oxygen Saturation

Oxygen level shows how much oxygen your blood is carrying.

Normal range: 95% - 100%

Respiratory Rate

Respiratory rate measures how many breaths you take per minute.

Normal resting range: 12 - 20 breaths/min

About Pulse

What is Pulse?

Pulse is a heart monitoring dashboard designed to help users track their cardiovascular health including BPM, oxygen levels, and respiratory rate.

Features

  • Real-time BPM tracking
  • Pulse history graph with Chart.js
  • Oxygen and respiratory rate overview
  • Custom profile management
  • Secure login system

Technology Used

Built using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Chart.js for data visualization.
